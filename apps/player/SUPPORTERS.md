# Kinosail supporters

Kinosail stays complete and private for everyone. These people chose to support
its continued development and also chose public recognition.

## Patron logbook

No public names yet.

Names and aliases appear here only after explicit consent. Patron levels 7–10
may request a listing through the optional public-name field in supporter
checkout. The project owner reviews and adds each entry manually. A supporter
may stay anonymous, use an alias, remove a listing later, and use every Kinosail
feature without appearing on this page.
