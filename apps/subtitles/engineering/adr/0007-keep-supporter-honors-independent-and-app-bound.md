# Keep supporter honors independent and app-bound

Kinosail Subtitles stores Patron Orders and Living Standards as independent, app-bound grants. Signed certificates define the badge family, exact level, expiry, and optional Complete Fleet scope. This avoids cumulative badge claims, prevents expired monthly support from appearing active, and permits public Share Certificates without private activation data.

## Consequences

A permanent Patron Order cannot replace a higher permanent level. A Living Standard can refresh or change while its signed expiry controls active status. Complete Fleet recognition comes only from signed collection metadata.
