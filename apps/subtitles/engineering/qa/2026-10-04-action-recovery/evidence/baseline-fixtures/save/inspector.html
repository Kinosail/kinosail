    Audio reference

    Analyze the audio on this Server to see where dialogue occurs throughout the video.

    Create a local subtitle draft

    Read text from image-based PGS/VobSub subtitles with OCR, or transcribe audio in the selected language. Both run on this Server. Review the generated text before saving.

    OCR needs the language's Tesseract data. Transcription needs a local Whisper model in configuration. One draft is kept at a time; starting another replaces it. Drafts are cleared when the server restarts.

    Recognition confidence and estimated word timing

    Highlighted words may need correction. Word times are estimated for transcripts; words read from subtitle images share the timing of their subtitle line.

    Prepare a change

    SRT, WebVTT, ASS/SSA, or TTML · up to 4 MB. The original is retained when you save.

    Choose captions only after checking sound descriptions and speaker information. A generated transcript does not automatically include this information.

    Automatic sync and text cleanup are on for previews. Review the result before saving. If audio cannot be matched, turn off sync or use manual timing.

    Timing anchors and cleanup

    Positive values show subtitles later. Use anchors to correct drift; choose either a shift or anchors.

    Correct subtitle text

    Load the current preview, edit its numbered SRT cues, and preview again. Loading the preview resets timing controls because these timestamps already include your changes.

    Saving keeps a recovery copy and protects your edit from automatic upgrades.
